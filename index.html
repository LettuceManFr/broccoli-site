What's inside

Everything, in green.

Broccoli Client runs Minecraft in your browser and on your desktop, with a fresh look and modules built for the servers you actually play.

Fresh green GUI

A redesigned modules menu with a sidebar, logo card, smooth scrolling and an animated gradient background.

Price Book

Reads item sell prices on economy servers and shows how much to sell for a million, with change alerts and farm tips.

Bridging

Reach-around block placement for faster bridging, for servers that allow it.

Built-in music

Bloom on the menus and Memory Reboot in game, always looping, baked into every build.

Fast & smooth

Tuned for quick chunk loading and the smoothest frames the build can give.
